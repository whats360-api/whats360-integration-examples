# CRM settings: conversation statuses (dashboard)

Source: whats360.live dashboard export dated 2026-05 (p47), read at full size. Dashboard feature, login required, not an API. Nothing here was run by us, and no CRM API is shown on the page.

## What the page shows
- A device selector at the top and two tabs: conversation statuses and customer types. The statuses tab was open.
- A table of statuses with these columns: colour, English name, Arabic name, order, a "system" tag and action icons (lock and edit).
- Ten statuses were listed, in this order: Pending, Open, In Progress, Waiting Reply, Follow Up, Priority, Completed, Archived, Cancelled, Spam. Each has an Arabic name and a colour dot.
- "Add new status" opens a form with four fields: English name (internal key, for example new or closed), Arabic name, colour and order. Buttons: Create and Cancel.

## Notes
- The auto-status rules page (see bots-dashboard.md) also lists "Inactive". It was not on this page, so the lists may differ by account or by date. Check your own dashboard.
- The customer types tab was not open on this page and is not documented here.
- No API, webhook or export for statuses is documented.
